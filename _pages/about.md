I'm a postdoctoral researcher at Northeastern University. I got my PhD at the University of Chicago working with [Blase Ur](https://www.blaseur.com/) and was supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. I graduated from Morehouse College in 2021 with a BS in Computer Science.

My research focuses on understanding how and why people experience harms and challenges from personalized advertising, social media feeds, AI chatbots, and other algorithmic systems.
Most recently, I've been investigating how and why people use search on TikTok and how algorithmic features affect how people use TikTok.
